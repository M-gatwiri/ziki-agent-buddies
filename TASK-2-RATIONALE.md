# Task 2 rationale

- One small, attentive helper makes Ziki feel present without crowding the work.
- Its eyes follow a mouse pointer with a subtle, bounded movement.
- A tap, click, or keyboard activation earns a brief sparkle and warmer smile.
- The soft blue, warm white, and muted gold palette keeps the character calm and professional.
- The mascot is a native button with a clear accessible name, focus ring, and live reaction announcement.
- Touch users can activate the same greeting; pointer tracking is simply absent without a mouse.
- Reduced-motion settings remove the sparkle transition while preserving the greeting response.
- Inline SVG and a few CSS rules keep the page responsive and lightweight, with no animation library or service calls.
