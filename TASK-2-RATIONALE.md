# Task 2 rationale

- Four friendly agent buddies make working, completed, attention, and idle states easy to recognize.
- Rounded silhouettes, gentle expressions, and distinct palettes add delight without making the workspace feel childish.
- Navy surfaces and Ziki blue accents connect the concept to Ziki’s human + AI product language.
- Each card summarizes a sample task; selecting it reveals a short, clear note from that buddy.
- The wave action adds a small moment of interaction without implying control of a real agent.
- Native buttons, visible focus rings, text labels, and a live announcement support keyboard and assistive-technology use.
- Reduced-motion preferences shorten animations, and layouts collapse from four columns to two and then one.
- Inline vector art and CSS keep the experience lightweight; all activity is local sample data with no service calls.
